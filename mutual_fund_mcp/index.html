Features

Fund comparison

SIP analysis

Advanced example · multi-step analysis

Go beyond a single question.

One natural-language request can combine fund discovery, NAV history, comparative performance, risk measures, SIP modelling, and portfolio tools.

Show a complex research and portfolio prompt

Compare Parag Parikh Flexi Cap, SBI Contra, and HDFC Flexi Cap (all Direct, Growth) over the last 10 years. For each fund, give me:

  1. 3Y and 5Y rolling return summary (min, median, max)
  2. Maximum drawdown, with the peak, trough, and recovery date
  3. Sharpe, Sortino, and volatility
  4. Calendar-year returns, and how many years each beat the others
  5. Result of a ₹10K monthly SIP from Jan 2017, with XIRR

Then rank the funds for (a) a conservative investor and (b) an aggressive investor, with reasoning from the numbers. Finish with one table summarising everything.

Add these transactions to my portfolio: ₹50,000 lumpsum in PPFAS Direct on 15 Jan 2021, ₹30,000 lumpsum in SBI Contra Direct on 10 Mar 2022, and ₹40,000 in HDFC Flexi Cap Direct on 5 Aug 2023. Then show my current portfolio value, allocation by fund house, XIRR, and which holding is dragging returns the most.

The MCP calculates and compares historical data; it does not give personalized investment advice. Portfolio transaction examples change saved portfolio data when invoked. Results depend on source history, exact scheme selection, and available dates.

Why this MCP

Source-dated numbers, transparent calculations.

This MCP connects your AI client to MFAPI.in for mutual-fund scheme data and published NAV history. Calculations use the dated observations returned by the source; NAV values are not generated or interpolated.

✳Historical NAVs keep their actual source dates, with available coverage from the earliest records returned for each scheme.
✳Results include observation counts, NAV date ranges, cache status, and calculation assumptions for easier review.
✳Analytics aid research; they are not investment recommendations. MFAPI does not provide fund holdings, so stock-level overlap is unavailable.
